# College Student Budget Tracker

## Student Name
Kaylie

## App Description
This app will help college students manage their money in one place. Users will be able to record money coming in (inflows) and going out (outflows), track recurring bills like rent and utilities, and set money aside into savings. The app will show a clear summary of where their money is going each month.

## Intended Users
This app is intended for college students who are managing their own money for the first time and want a simple way to stay on top of income, bills, and savings goals.

## Planned Features
- Log inflows (paychecks, allowance, financial aid) and outflows (food, transportation, entertainment)
- Track recurring payments such as rent and utilities with due dates
- Savings tool for moving money into a savings balance and setting a savings goal
- Monthly summary with charts showing spending by category and remaining balance
